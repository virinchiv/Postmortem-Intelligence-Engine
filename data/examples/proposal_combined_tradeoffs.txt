System Design Proposal: Combined Performance Tradeoffs

Overview:
This proposal combines multiple optimizations: retries, shared caching, rolling deployments, and reduced logging.

Design:
- Microservices use bounded retries.
- Shared cache reduces database pressure.
- Rolling deploys minimize downtime.
- Logging is optimized for signal over volume.

Assumptions:
- System is mature and well-monitored.
- Team understands operational tradeoffs.

Risks:
- Increased system complexity.
- Hidden failure modes interacting together.

Mitigations:
- Regular postmortems.
- Automated testing and staged rollouts.
