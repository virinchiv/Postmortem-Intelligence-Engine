System Design Proposal: Microservices with Retries

Overview:
This proposal describes a microservices-based architecture where services communicate over HTTP/gRPC and include retry mechanisms for transient failures.

Design:
- Each microservice is independently deployable and stateless.
- Inter-service communication uses timeouts and bounded retries.
- Retries are implemented with exponential backoff and jitter.
- Circuit breakers are used to prevent cascading failures.

Assumptions:
- Failures are mostly transient (network blips, brief overloads).
- Services can tolerate eventual consistency.

Risks:
- Retry storms under heavy load.
- Increased latency due to retries.

Mitigations:
- Strict retry limits.
- Centralized observability to detect retry amplification.
