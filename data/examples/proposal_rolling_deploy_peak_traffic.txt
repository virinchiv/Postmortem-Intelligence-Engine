System Design Proposal: Rolling Deployment During Peak Traffic

Overview:
This proposal outlines a rolling deployment strategy executed while the system is under peak user traffic.

Design:
- Services are deployed incrementally across instances.
- Load balancer gradually shifts traffic to new versions.
- Health checks gate progression of the rollout.

Assumptions:
- Backward compatibility between versions.
- Strong monitoring is in place.

Risks:
- Partial outages if bugs exist.
- Increased error rates during rollout.

Mitigations:
- Canary deployments.
- Immediate rollback triggers based on SLOs.
