System Design Proposal: Reduced Logging for Performance

Overview:
This proposal reduces logging verbosity to improve system performance and lower storage costs.

Design:
- Only warnings, errors, and critical events are logged in production.
- Debug logs are disabled by default.
- Sampling is applied to high-frequency logs.

Assumptions:
- Metrics and traces cover most observability needs.
- Detailed debugging is rare in production.

Risks:
- Loss of diagnostic information.
- Harder root-cause analysis.

Mitigations:
- On-demand log level escalation.
- Strong metrics and tracing coverage.
